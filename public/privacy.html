Privacy policy

Airlie Beach Art only collects the information needed to fulfil your order and, if you choose, keep you updated. I never sell your information.

Who I am

Airlie Beach Art is run by Michael van Blerk, a sole trader based in Airlie Beach, Queensland, Australia. Contact: michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and order details.
  • Payments: card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed securely by Square. I never see or store your full card details.
  • When you join the mailing list: your name and email address.
  • When you enquire: the details you include in your message or the enquiry form, such as your name, email, subject or reference painting, size, budget, timeline and country.
  • When you visit the site: anonymous usage statistics, such as pages viewed, the country visits come from and how many listings are clicked or added to a cart, to help me improve the site. Your IP address is also used to block automated abuse and is recorded in security logs.

How I use it

  • To process, pack and deliver your order, and contact you about it.
  • To respond to enquiries and commission requests.
  • To send updates about new work and availability, only if you've joined the mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the site and its payments secure.

Who I share it with

I only share what's needed to complete your order and run the site:

  • Payment processing: Square, and Apple or Google if you pay with their wallets.
  • Printing: my printing partner in Mackay, Queensland, for print orders.
  • Delivery: Australia Post and courier companies.
  • Customs: Zonos, for orders to the United States, to prepare customs and duty paperwork.
  • Running the site: Vercel (hosting and visitor statistics), Upstash (database), Resend (delivers order, enquiry and mailing list notifications to me) and Google Fonts (which receives your IP address when the site's fonts load).

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

How I keep it safe

Your information is stored with reputable service providers that use secure systems, and is sent over encrypted connections.

  • Orders stay in the site's password-protected database for 90 days. I keep order records for as long as needed for tax and legal purposes, then delete them.
  • Enquiries and mailing list signups reach me by email, and I keep them for as long as they're needed.
  • Security logs record IP addresses and some activity, such as the name on an enquiry. Only the latest 500 entries are kept.

Browser storage

Your browser stores a note that you've seen the mailing list popup, and the contents of your cart until you close the tab. I don't use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or unsubscribe from emails, at any time by emailing michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com. If you're in the UK or EU, you also have rights under UK and EU data protection law.

If you have a privacy concern, contact me first and I'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
