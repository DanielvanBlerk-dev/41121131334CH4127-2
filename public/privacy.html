Privacy policy

Airlie Beach Art only collects the information needed to fulfil your order and, if you choose, keep you updated. I never sell your information.

Who I am What I collect How I use it Who I share it with How I keep it safe Your rights Changes

Who I am

Airlie Beach Art is run by Michael van Blerk, a sole trader based in Airlie Beach, Queensland, Australia. Contact: michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com.

What I collect

When you order

Your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and order details.

Payments

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed securely by Square. I never see or store your full card details.

When you join the mailing list

Your name and email address.

When you enquire

The details you include in your message or the enquiry form.

When you visit the site

Anonymous usage statistics, such as pages viewed and the country visits come from, to help me improve the site. Your IP address is also used to block automated abuse and is recorded in security logs. Your browser stores a note that you've seen the mailing list popup, and your cart until you close the tab. I don't use advertising cookies.

How I use it

  • To process, pack and deliver your order, and contact you about it.
  • To respond to enquiries and commission requests.
  • To send updates about new work and availability, only if you've joined the mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who I share it with

I only share what's needed to complete your order and run the site:

PurposeProvider
Payment processingSquare, plus Apple or Google for wallet payments
PrintingMy printing partner in Mackay, Queensland, for print orders
DeliveryAustralia Post and courier companies
CustomsZonos, for orders to the United States, to prepare customs and duty paperwork
Running the siteVercel (hosting and statistics), Upstash (database), Resend (email notifications) and Google Fonts (receives your IP address when fonts load)

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

How I keep it safe

Your information is stored with reputable service providers that use secure systems. Orders stay in the site's password-protected database for 90 days, and security logs keep only the latest 500 entries. I keep order records for as long as needed for tax and legal purposes, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or unsubscribe from emails, at any time by emailing michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com. If you're in the UK or EU, you also have rights under UK and EU data protection law.

If you have a privacy concern, contact me first and I'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
