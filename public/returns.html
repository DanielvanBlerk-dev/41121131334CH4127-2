Returns and damage

If your artwork arrives damaged, I'll put it right. I don't accept change-of-mind returns.

  1. Email me within 7 daysSend michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com photos of the damage, the packaging and the shipping label, within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Keep all the packagingThe carrier may need it for the insurance claim.

Commissions

Commissioned paintings are made to your brief, so they can't be returned for change of mind. You approve the sketch and colour plan before painting starts, and you see the finished painting before the balance is paid. See the Commissions page for details.

Your rights under Australian law

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If an item has a major problem, you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund.
