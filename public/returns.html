Returns and damage

If your artwork arrives damaged, I'll put it right. I don't accept change-of-mind returns.

If your artwork arrives damaged

  1. Email michael.p.vanblerk@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the damage, the packaging and the shipping label.
  2. Keep all the packaging. The carrier may need it for the insurance claim.
  3. I'll arrange a replacement, repair, or a refund, depending on the damage and what you'd prefer.

Change of mind and satisfaction

I don't offer refunds for change of mind or satisfaction issues, such as a work not suiting your taste or space once you see it. If you'd like more photos or details before you buy, please ask me.

Commissions

Commissioned paintings are made to your brief, so they can't be returned for change of mind. You approve the sketch and colour plan before painting starts, and you see the finished painting before the balance is paid. See the Commissions page for details.

Your rights under Australian law

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If an item has a major problem, including being faulty or not as described, you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund.
