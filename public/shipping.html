Dispatch times

Original paintings

Dispatched within 5 business days of payment.

Order by Sunday night and your print ships that Friday. Order on Monday or later and it ships the Friday after. Please allow 1–2 weeks for prints to dispatch.

Commissions

Dispatched once the painting is finished and dry enough to travel safely. See the Commissions page.

Delivery estimates

After dispatch, typical delivery times are:

DestinationEstimated delivery

These are estimates from the carrier. Customs checks and holiday periods can add time.

Shipping costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your location and the size of the artwork. Large or oversized originals are sent by courier and quoted individually before you pay.

Packaging and insurance

Originals are wrapped with protective paper against the paint surface, then padded and boxed. Prints are sent flat between rigid board or rolled in a sturdy tube. Every shipment is tracked, and originals are insured for their full value.

If something arrives damaged, see Returns and damage.

Customs, duties and taxes

United States Any import duties and clearance fees are prepaid by me when your order ships, so there's nothing extra to pay on delivery. Original paintings are generally duty-free into the US.
All other countries outside Australia Your country may charge import taxes, duties or handling fees on delivery. These are set by your local customs authority and are paid by the buyer.

Local pickup and delivery

If you're in the Whitsundays, free pickup from the studio or local hand delivery can be arranged. Get in touch before ordering.
