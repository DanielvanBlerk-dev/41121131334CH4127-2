Shipping

Every painting and print is packed by hand and sent tracked and insured, from Airlie Beach in Queensland, Australia, to almost anywhere in the world.

Carriers

Paintings and prints are sent with Australia Post. Large or oversized originals are sent by courier.

Dispatch times

  • Original paintings: dispatched within 5 business days of payment.
  • Prints: printed to order on archival fine art paper by my printing partner in Mackay, Queensland. Print orders go to the printer every Monday for the previous week, usually come back by Wednesday, and are dispatched on Friday. Order by Sunday night and your print ships that Friday. Order on Monday or later and it ships the Friday after. Please allow 1–2 weeks for prints to dispatch.
  • Commissions: dispatched once the painting is finished and dry enough to travel safely. See the Commissions page.

Delivery estimates (after dispatch)

DestinationEstimated delivery

These are estimates from the carrier. Customs checks and holiday periods can add time.

Shipping costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your location and the size of the artwork. Large or oversized originals are sent by courier and quoted individually before you pay.

Packaging, insurance and tracking

Originals are wrapped with protective paper against the paint surface, then padded and boxed. Prints are sent flat between rigid board or rolled in a sturdy tube. Every shipment is tracked and insured for its full value, at no extra cost to you. If something arrives damaged, see Returns and damage.

Customs, duties and taxes

  • United States: any import duties and clearance fees are prepaid by me when your order ships, so there's nothing extra to pay on delivery. Original paintings are generally duty-free into the US.
  • All other countries outside Australia: your country may charge import taxes, duties or handling fees on delivery. These are set by your local customs authority and are paid by the buyer.

Local pickup and delivery

If you're in the Whitsundays, free pickup from the studio or local hand delivery can be arranged. Get in touch before ordering.
