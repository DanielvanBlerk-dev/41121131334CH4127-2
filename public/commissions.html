What I paint

Seascape Commissions

Bring a photograph, a location, or simply a mood you'd like captured, and I'll paint it. Quoted based on size and complexity.

Figurative & Devotional Commissions

Sacred subjects, theological narratives, portraits in the classical tradition. Each commission is discussed individually to ensure the work serves its intended space and purpose. Ecclesiastical commissions particularly welcome.

How it works

  1. EnquireTell me the subject, the size you have in mind, your timeline and your country, using the enquiry form or by email.
  2. QuoteI'll send a quote with size options, the painting price and shipping, so you know the full cost up front.
  3. DepositA 50% non-refundable deposit secures your spot. Paying the deposit means you accept these terms.
  4. Sketch and colour planBefore I start painting, I send you a sketch and colour plan to approve. Two rounds of changes are included at this stage.
  5. PaintingI'll send progress photos along the way.
  6. Approval and balanceWhen it's finished, I send photos of the completed painting. The remaining 50% is due once you're happy.
  7. ShippingOnce it's dry enough to travel, it's packed and sent tracked and insured, with a signed certificate of authenticity.

Timelines

Most commissions take 6–8 weeks from sketch approval to dispatch, depending on size and how many are in the queue. Oil paint needs drying time before it can be shipped safely, so larger or more layered paintings take longer. If you need it by a particular date, tell me when you enquire.

Revisions

  • Two rounds of changes to the sketch and colour plan are included.
  • Further sketch revisions are A$40 each.
  • Once painting has begun, small adjustments are fine. Major changes to the composition or colours incur a fee of 10% of the quoted price, payable before the changes are made.

Deposits and cancellations

The deposit covers materials and the studio time reserved for your painting, so it isn't refundable if you cancel. If I'm unable to complete your commission for any reason, your deposit will be refunded in full.

Copyright

You own the original painting. I keep the copyright, which means I may photograph, film and share the painting and its creation, and reproduce it as prints. If you'd prefer your painting kept private or not reproduced, let me know before we start. This is available for an additional fee, which I'll quote up front.
